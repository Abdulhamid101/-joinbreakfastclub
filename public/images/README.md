# Where photos go

Drop files in with these exact names and they appear on the live site
automatically — no code changes needed.

## hero/cover.jpg
Full-bleed, golden hour, a quiet Abeokuta road. A small group mid-stride,
backs partly to camera, breath visible in the cool morning air. No branding
on the runners. Candid and warm — closer to an early lululemon lookbook
crossed with a 90s Nike print ad, but unmistakably Nigerian in light, skin
tones, dress, and setting. (Shown at low opacity behind the headline —
doesn't need to be a finished, color-graded hero shot.)

## audience/member-1.jpg – member-4.jpg
Candid portraits across ages and occupations, shot in the same light and
location as the cover, so the set reads as one community rather than a
stock photo montage. Roughly portrait orientation (3:4).

## story/ (not wired into the site yet — for the next pass)
First light on an empty road · a small group stretching · a shared flask
of tea · a plate of moin moin on a fold-out table · the same road at full
daylight, now crowded. Meant to narrate growth without any chart or stat.

## personas/ (not wired into the site yet — for the next pass)
One unposed portrait per persona (Tola, Uncle Femi, Blessing, Chidi) —
avoid stock photography or anything overly polished; these should look
like real members, because they are.

Formats: .jpg or .png, ideally under 500KB each (compress before
uploading — TinyPNG or squoosh.app both work) so the site stays fast.
